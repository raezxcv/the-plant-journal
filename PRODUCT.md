# PRODUCT.md — The Plant Journal

## Product Summary
**The Plant Journal** (*An Ongoing Study of Things That Grow*) is an independent quarterly botanical publication and digital herbarium documenting cultivated flora, glasshouse architecture, indoor conservatory ecology, and the quiet reciprocity between people and living plants.

## Core Positioning
- **Category**: Independent Botanical Publication & Digital Field Guide (Read + Experience surface)
- **What it is NOT**: A commercial plant shop, e-commerce nursery, or generic gardening blog. There are no prices, shopping carts, or promotional discount popups.
- **Audience**: Amateur botanists, urban horticulturalists, landscape designers, architectural conservators, and readers of tactile print journals (such as *Kinfolk*, *Cereal*, *Cabana*, and Royal Botanic Gardens Kew monographs).

## Surface Architecture (4 Distinct Pages)
1. **Home (`/`)**: Issue No. 14 cover dispatch featuring the glasshouse monograph hero, the lead botanical essay, recent herbarium accessions, seasonal phenology notes, and the publication colophon/intro.
2. **Plant Index (`/index`)**: A structured taxonomic ledger and specimen grid of 15 documented botanical species with binomial nomenclature, botanical families, light/water/humidity profiles, cultivation difficulty, live search, multi-axis filtering, view mode switching (Specimen Grid vs. Taxonomic Ledger), and local Herbarium Bookmarking.
3. **Botanical Profile (`/plants/:slug`)**: Deep monograph page for each plant specimen—featuring natural-light macro photography, taxonomic classification table, geographic origin & habitat narrative, morphological description, growing conditions metrics, expandable seasonal care protocols, and related taxa links.
4. **Journal & About (`/journal`)**: Long-form editorial essays on directional window light, vegetative propagation, small-space conservatory design, botanical macro photography, and the publication's editorial manifesto, masthead, and reader dispatch subscription.
